Business assumptions: Price stays $2 per can. Year 1 starts with $10B revenue, $1B profit, $600M marketing, and $1.10 cost per can. Without intervention, annual sales decline and costs rise.

Valuation check

Classroom valuation rule: enterprise value equals the sum of the five forecast years' profits. A negative result signals cumulative projected losses and financial distress, not a literal negative stock price. Real-world enterprise valuation considers cash flows, risk, and the time value of money.
